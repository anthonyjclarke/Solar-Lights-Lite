Your BTE13-010A and available parts

The photographed BTE13-010A is the chosen controller and its fuses have been read back as internal 8 MHz, BOD 2.7 V and no bootloader. Q2 is selected as 2N3904. The measured string runs at about 15 mA straight from D9 through R3, with no Q1 fitted.

The starting point: dusk to dawn at the measured 15 mA

The string was measured on 20-Sep-2026: a 2.56 V drop with no significant dynamic resistance, so the series resistor alone sets the current. With R3 = 47 ohm the branch draws about 21, 15 and 11 mA at 4.20, 3.70 and 3.40 V, and runs straight from D9 with no MOSFET. Commanded duty is now brightness only, so the firmware runs 100% dusk to dawn, halving below 3.45 V and turning off after 30 seconds below 3.30 V. Startup and button tests obey the same cap.

At 14 dark hours the illustrative whole-system estimate is about 238 mAh/day at full brightness, or about 133 mAh/day at half. These are scenarios built on one bench measurement and an assumed charge current, not measured performance. Cell capacity, actual shade and usable brightness still decide whether the design meets your needs.

Diagnostic firmware settings and programming isolation

Build diagnostics with pio run -e pro8_debug; it enables DEBUG_SERIAL=1, keeps real-time TIME_SCALE=1, and reports at 9600 baud. On every reset, a one-time field guide explains the names, units, thresholds, modes, timers and report cadence before live diagnostics begin. Reports are immediate for important changes, every 10 seconds during active confirmation/test/cutoff timing, at 10-percentage-point fade changes, and every 60 seconds when stable. The v0.5 D1 Mini test profile remains awake and publishes those data in Home Assistant; use pio run -e pro8 and the low-power D1 profile for final sleep-current testing. This controller has no bootloader: upload only with the separately installed current avrdude, not pio -t upload. Fully disconnect the D1 Mini receiver, battery, panel and LED string before attaching the programmer; reconnect battery power and the receiver only after programming is complete. The exact macOS, Windows, USBasp and Arduino-as-ISP commands are kept in the single firmware programming procedure.

Validation status

Both legacy KiCad sheets export the same 25 nets. Their ERC reports contain 1 error each and 167 / 169 warnings, mostly off-grid drawing endpoints. Matching netlists do not validate the underlying circuit.

The current scheduler passes nine assertion groups, including dusk debounce, a 14-hour night, the shipped 100% default, low-battery dimming and low-voltage recovery. The BTE13-010A fuse readback confirms internal 8 MHz, BOD 2.7 V and no bootloader; earlier application firmware was written and verified through ISP. v0.5 adds a test-only D1 Mini UART monitor, but it does not create current, energy, panel-voltage or temperature measurements. The staged measurements above remain required to qualify the charger, solar input, physical LED current and complete assembly.

A future low-cost PCB should first be a module carrier using the exact proven parts. The old KiCad files are retained for audit and must not be sent for fabrication.
