Your BTE13-010A and available parts

The photographed BTE13-010A is the chosen controller and its fuses have been read back as internal 8 MHz, BOD 2.7 V and no bootloader. Q2 is selected as 2N3904. The measured string runs at about 15 mA straight from D9 through R3, with no Q1 fitted.

The starting point: dusk to dawn at the measured 15 mA

The string was measured on 20 September: a 2.56 V drop with no significant dynamic resistance, so the series resistor alone sets the current. With R3 = 47 ohm the branch draws about 21, 15 and 11 mA at 4.20, 3.70 and 3.40 V, and runs straight from D9 with no MOSFET. Commanded duty is now brightness only, so the firmware runs 100% dusk to dawn, halving below 3.45 V and turning off after 30 seconds below 3.30 V. Startup and button tests obey the same cap.

At 14 dark hours the illustrative whole-system estimate is about 238 mAh/day at full brightness, or about 133 mAh/day at half. These are scenarios built on one bench measurement and an assumed charge current, not measured performance. Cell capacity, actual shade and usable brightness still decide whether the design meets your needs.

05 / Staged testing and serial diagnostics

Stop and test after every stage. Do not assemble the complete battery, charger, solar input and controller and then apply power for the first time. Each row below is an acceptance gate: proceed only after the measured result passes.

Never connect two positive power sources to the Pro Mini. Once the protected battery rail supplies VCC, remove the USB–UART adapter's VCC/3V3/5V lead. For serial monitoring use only adapter RX, adapter GND and USB power to the adapter.

Open full-size SVG · PNG preview · the USB-UART pin order follows the supplied board photograph

Use OUT- / GND_LOAD for the serial ground, never B-. Verify that the adapter RX input tolerates a Pro Mini TX high level up to the full-cell voltage (about 4.2 V). Keep the USB adapter powered whenever TX is connected, or disconnect TX so an unpowered adapter cannot be back-fed through its RX pin.

Serial does not validate solar charging. The firmware measures its own VCC and the LDR only. A0 panel sensing and the charger's CHRG signal are unused, so validate panel voltage, regulated 5 V and charge current with instruments.
Diagnostic firmware settings and programming isolation

Build diagnostics with pio run -e pro8_debug; it enables DEBUG_SERIAL=1, keeps real-time TIME_SCALE=1, and monitors at 9600 baud. On every reset, a one-time field guide explains the names, units, thresholds, modes, timers and report cadence before live diagnostics begin. Reports are immediate for important changes, every 10 seconds during active confirmation/test/cutoff timing, at 10-percentage-point fade changes, and every 60 seconds when stable. Serial adds roughly 1 mA; use pio run -e pro8 for final sleep-current testing and deployment. This controller has no bootloader: upload only with the separately installed current avrdude, not pio -t upload. Fully disconnect the UART adapter, battery, panel and LED string before attaching the programmer; reconnect battery power and the receive-only UART monitor only after programming is complete. The exact macOS, Windows, USBasp and Arduino-as-ISP commands are kept in the single firmware programming procedure.

Validation status

Both legacy KiCad sheets export the same 25 nets. Their ERC reports contain 1 error each and 167 / 169 warnings, mostly off-grid drawing endpoints. Matching netlists do not validate the underlying circuit.

The current scheduler passes nine assertion groups, including dusk debounce, a 14-hour night, the shipped 100% default, low-battery dimming and low-voltage recovery. The BTE13-010A fuse readback confirms internal 8 MHz, BOD 2.7 V and no bootloader; the Rev 0.4 application firmware was subsequently written and verified through ISP. Serial output and the D2 test provide controller-level evidence, but the staged measurements above are still required to qualify the charger, solar input, physical LED current and complete assembly.

A future low-cost PCB should first be a module carrier using the exact proven parts. The old KiCad files are retained for audit and must not be sent for fabrication.
