Solar Front Lights · Lite · Rev 0.2 · 18-Sep-2026

The parts-bin build

Same behaviour as v2 – dusk to ~22:30, pre-dawn, battery-aware dimming, low-battery cut-off and HA reporting – built from a TP4056 board, an Arduino Pro Mini, a logic-level MOSFET and your Wemos D1 Mini on perfboard. Stage 1 runs on your existing 1.2 W panel (20 % cap, evening only). Stage 2 swaps in a Waveshare 6 V 5 W panel (SKU 16158) if the winter tests call for it.

Wiring

OUT+ and OUT− from the TP4056 board become two perfboard bus strips, and every module taps off them.

Build order

Test each stage on the bench before adding the next. A current-limited supply stands in for the battery and the panel.

  1. Flash the Pro Mini with DEBUG_SERIAL 1, TIME_SCALE 60 and PANEL_STAGE 1 over a 3.3 V FTDI adapter. Check the serial output with the supply set to 3.8 V on VCC.
  2. Modify the Pro Mini: remove the power LED (or its resistor) and the regulator. Sleep current should fall to about 5–10 µA.
  3. Add the LED driver (MOSFET, 100 Ω, 100k, fuse, Rlim) and connect the string. Press the button: 30 % for 60 s.
  4. Add the panel divider on A0. Step the supply 6 V → 1 V → 3.5 V and confirm lights on and off after 5 "min".
  5. Wire the TP4056 board with the diodes. Feed 7 V through a 2–3 Ω resistor as a stand-in panel and confirm charging.
  6. Add the D1 Mini with the D0–RST link. Flash solar-lights-lite.yaml, then check the Battery, Lights On and Charging entities in HA.
  7. Reflash the Pro Mini with DEBUG_SERIAL 0 and TIME_SCALE 1, then fit the cells and box it up.
  8. Field-test Stage 1: 14 days normal, then 14 days with half the panel covered to emulate June. Upgrade to the 6 V 5 W panel and PANEL_STAGE 2 if the dusk battery voltage trends down or any low-battery night occurs.

MOSFET choice matters: a 2N7000 or BS170 drops about 1 V at 200 mA, so use an IRLZ44N, IRLB8721 or AO3400 from the parts box.
